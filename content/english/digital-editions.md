# Humanities Research

From expert advice on appropiate encoding standards, tools and workflows to final publication online or in print, datasets maintenance and integration with external systems - we can help you focus on the project while we handle the technical details. With a range of completed projects under our belt - covering virtually all periods and humanities research domains - and experience of our personnel we are uniquely positioned to suggest the best practices for you considering future-proof, state of the art solutions available.

## Encoding standards and data organization

Great projects start with good logistic. While often neglected, thorough analysis at the initial stage of the project can save a lot of heartache later. Specialist advice regarding the data modelling and organization, selection of applicable standards and preparation of encoding guidelines and schemas assures your project data stays consistent, ready for publication and research investigations for decades to come.

We can advise you on a range of metadata and text encoding standards, includig METS, MODS, DC, TEI, JATS and DocBook. We can also help setting up repositories in source control systems like Git as well as multi-user cloud environments like Airtables and ... to maintain authority files like lists of persons, places or bibliography. We will propose best fitting solutions to store additional resources, like image or binary files.
Consulting with us early on helps to avoid costs later as we can establish a data structure tailored to the specific requirements and research questions of a project.

# Digital Editions

## Transcription of the manuscript

Jinntec supports editions with different approaches depending on the customers requirements:

* create a Word template and customize the Word import accordingly to ensure high-quality TEI aligned with the established encoding guidelines
* XML-editing workflows based on oXygen or VSCode.
* or start with an HTR engine like transkribus for acquiring the raw transcription

All cases should include proper version management based on git."
    
## Semantic and analytic enrichment
This is usually the most time consuming task in a project. TEI Publisher's web annotations eliminate the tedious and repetitive work of encoding entities in the XML, so editors can concentrate on more interesting research instead of having to enter thousands of TEI elements for semantic and analytic markup.
    
## Metadata entry
Entering the necessary metadata about a document is often a separate workflow task. Knowing where exactly to put which information in the TEI header can be challenging at least: there are probably as many opinions as there are TEI users. We can free you from the burden by designing an input form which is customized for your requirements.

## Producing a Digital Edition

## Print Publication
Note: heavily neglected by us:

* documentation for print output is lacking
* there's no tutorial/extended description about how to generate high-quality LaTeX

But: we are able to produce PDF which can be directly passed to the printing press, see SSRQ or Buddhist Stone Sutras. And: we know how to automate the print production process to avoid any labour intensive, manual typesetting and thus reduce costs tremendously.
