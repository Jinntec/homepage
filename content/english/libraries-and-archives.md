## long-term availability of editions and publications
when the funding for a project ends after 2 or 3 years, libraries are expected to take care of the long-term hosting. In practice this is often an impossible challenge: electronic editions age as rapidly as the software used to build them. By largely relying on approved standards, TEI Publisher tries to address this issue. Editions powered by TEI Publisher may look differently on the outside, but under the hood they all follow the same structure, use the same standards and building blocks for the user interface and presentation (also see the e-editiones agenda).
## sustainability and reduction of costs
TEI Publisher enables institutions to produce digital editions quickly, following the always same principles. A small, trained team can efficiently customize and maintain a large number of editions, tailoring each to match the edition's specific requirements as necessary, but in a standardized way.

## interoperability and open access
editions created by TEI Publisher are interoperable by design. An institution can use our portal application to incorporate multiple editions, providing convenient access for researchers as well as increase visibility.
